36-word pronunciation page demo.
Open index.html in a browser to test.
The pages use your device browser speech synthesis, not recorded MP3 files. Voice quality and gender depend on installed voices.
For printed QR codes, upload play.html and index.html to a stable HTTPS hosting address. Then create each QR code with the full address: https://YOUR-DOMAIN/play.html?word=literate etc.
Do not print QR codes before verifying hosted URLs and playback on a phone.
